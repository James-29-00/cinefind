How Cinefind works

It only links to other websites. Nothing is hosted or streamed here.

  1. Find a websiteSearch a title, or browse the websites by category.
  2. Open itTap a website, then Visit to open it in a new tab.
  3. Save favoritesTap the bookmark on a website to keep it. Saved websites stay on this device only.
  4. Check the statusThe status shows whether a website was reachable at its last check.
What the status meansOnline: ReachableOffline: Unavailable after retryFlagged: CAPTCHA / robot verificationUnknown: Indeterminate

An automated check can’t confirm a title is available. Please check each website yourself.
