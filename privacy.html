Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Cinefind is a directory of third-party streaming websites. We do not require an account and we do not ask for your name or email.

What we collect

  • Anonymous session ID. Used only to count visits and how many people are online. It is not linked to your identity.
  • Search terms. When you search for a title, the query is sent to our servers and to third-party services that provide title information and site suggestions.
  • Reports. If you report a website, the details you submit are sent to us.

What stays on your device

Favorites, recent searches, your theme choice and a short-lived cache of trending titles are stored in your browser. They never leave your device and you can clear them in your browser settings.

Third-party services and websites

Title data and posters come from TMDB. Cinefind is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you click Visit, you leave Cinefind and the other website's own privacy practices apply. We do not control those sites, and they may show ads or pop-ups.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use advertising cookies or sell your data.
