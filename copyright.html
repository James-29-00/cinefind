Copyright notices

Cinefind is a directory of links to other websites. If one of those links points to material that infringes your copyright, tell us and we will review it.

What Cinefind is

Cinefind does not host, upload or store any movies, shows or files. It lists links to third-party websites that we do not own or control, so we cannot remove content from those websites. What we can do is remove our link to them.

How to send a notice

Email the address below. We accept notices under the Intellectual Property Code of the Philippines (Republic Act No. 8293, as amended), and we also accept the format rights holders commonly use internationally. Please include:

  • The work you own, or are authorized to act for, that you believe is being infringed.
  • The exact Cinefind page or link you want reviewed, with its URL.
  • Your name, address, phone number and email address.
  • A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorized by the owner, the owner's agent or the law.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.
  • Your signature. A typed name is fine.

What happens next

We aim to review every complete notice within 3 business days. If the notice is complete and the claim looks valid, we remove the link and let you know. If something is missing, we will write back and ask for it.

If you think a link was removed by mistake, email the same address with the details and we will look again.

Before you send

Please send a notice only if you own the work or are authorized to act for the owner. Sending a false notice can have legal consequences.
